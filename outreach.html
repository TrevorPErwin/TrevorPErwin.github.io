Outreach

Sharing astronomy and science with students, educators, and the broader community through public events, demonstrations, and educational engagement.

Public Observing Nights

Guiding community members through telescope observations of the Moon, planets, star clusters, and deep-sky objects. These events provide hands-on opportunities for visitors to experience astronomy firsthand while learning about the science behind what they observe.

K–12 STEM Outreach
